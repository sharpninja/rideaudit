# Bill of materials

License: GPL-2.0

## Printed parts

| Part | Qty | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Headrest cross-beam with sliding post clamps | 1 | From `headrest-phone-mount.scad` (monolithic default) |
| Phone cradle (left) | 1 | Included when `dual_cradle=true`; else single center cradle |
| Phone cradle (right) | 0 or 1 | Second cradle when dual |

The default model is a single printable assembly. Split into pieces in OpenSCAD later if your bed is small.

## Optional hardware

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Soft foam or rubber pad strips | 2 to 4 | Between cradle and phone; reduces slip and vibration |
| M3x12 socket cap screws | 2 to 4 | Optional lock for sliding clamp if not relying on friction alone |
| M3 hex nuts or heat-set inserts | 2 to 4 | Match screws if used |
| Velcro / reusable cable tie | 1 to 2 | Secure charge cables in routing notch |

## Not included

- Phones, chargers, or vehicle electrical taps
- Adhesives on airbag covers or headliner (do not use)
