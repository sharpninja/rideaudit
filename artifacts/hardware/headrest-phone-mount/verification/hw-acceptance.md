This record is the Bracket plan's closure for the physical-fit and notice checks. It does not close FR-RIDE-041's software criteria (device identity, attestation, timestamps). Those stay with the Android plan.

The mount is no longer a dual cradle on a rail. It is one shared landscape cradle cut for a closed Galaxy Z Fold 4. Two post blocks carry horizontal 200 mm arms into that cradle from the rear. HW-AC-MOUNT-001 is applied to that single cradle (closed Fold 4 envelope, primary cameras facing forward). HW-AC-MOUNT-002 is applied to the two independent blocks over `post_spacing_min`..`post_spacing_max`, with a parametric post bore. Simultaneous two-phone cradles are not part of this design. This file does not record an on-vehicle print.

CAD evidence is [geometry-report.md](geometry-report.md), regenerated by `verify-geometry.py`. On-vehicle evidence is the unchecked list in [vehicle-fit-checklist.md](vehicle-fit-checklist.md). This package is **not** claimed as a road release.
